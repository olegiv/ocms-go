# oCMS accessibility

We aim to make oCMS usable with a keyboard, at increased zoom, and with assistive technology. This statement covers the built-in admin interface and the shipped Default and Developer themes.

## Scope and known limitations

- The admin and shipped themes run in desktop and mobile web browsers. Formal WCAG conformance and specific browser and assistive technology combinations are unverified.
- Reordering fields in the admin form builder uses drag and drop.
- The accessibility of a published site also depends on its theme, content, and third-party embeds.

## Report an accessibility barrier

Open a [bug report](https://github.com/olegiv/ocms-go/issues/new/choose) and include **Accessibility** in the title. Tell us what you were trying to do, where the barrier occurred, and the steps to reproduce it. If relevant, include your browser, operating system, input method, and assistive technology name and version.
