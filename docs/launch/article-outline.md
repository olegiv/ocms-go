## Proposed structure

- **The editor's task:** start with a brief and selected site references;
  generate the text in a connected AI application, then save a reviewable draft.
- **A concrete walkthrough:** an English synthetic article, a focused edit in
  another app and two linked translations. Show page/revision IDs and issues,
  rather than claiming a save from generated text alone.
- **Grounding and editorial guidance:** visible source revisions, existing
  taxonomy/media, presets and the difference between structural validation
  and factual checking.
- **Continuing safely:** own-draft access, revision conflicts, retained retry
  keys and translation freshness. Use actual acceptance results for examples.
- **Review in oCMS:** recorded principal/app, validated references and the
  agent's unverified reports; per-revision claim acknowledgments and diffs.
- **Previewing a snapshot:** session previews by default; explicit expiring
  fragment links only when sharing is requested. Explain revocation and the
  body-only redemption transport without exposing a real bearer.
- **Managing access:** Account → Connected apps, registered app metadata,
  scopes and revocation; administrator oversight and bounded OAuth retention.
- **Publication:** the human editor reviews and publishes. Distinguish local
  implementation, measured staging acceptance and release work still pending.
