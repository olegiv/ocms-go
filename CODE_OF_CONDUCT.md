# oCMS Code of Conduct

oCMS welcomes people of all backgrounds, identities, and experience levels. Everyone should be able to participate without harassment or discrimination.

## Community standards

- Be respectful of other contributors and their experiences.
- Give constructive feedback and focus disagreements on the work.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and listen when someone raises a concern.
- Respect people's privacy. Get permission before sharing information they provided in private.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing private information without consent violate these standards. Retaliating against someone for reporting a concern also violates these standards.

## Scope

This code applies in oCMS project spaces, including issues, pull requests, Discussions, and the wiki. It also applies when someone officially represents oCMS in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Send conduct reports privately to [conduct@ocms.tech](mailto:conduct@ocms.tech). Include a description, relevant links or screenshots, and any immediate concerns. Share sensitive details only if you are comfortable doing so.

Maintainers will review reports fairly and limit access to identifying details to the people who need them to respond. Depending on the severity and history of the behavior, maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
